Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 September 2026

1. Who We Are

This Privacy Policy explains how ARIES GROUP (“ARIES”, “we”, “us” or “our”) collects, uses and protects personal data when you visit our website or contact us through it. ARIES GROUP is the controller of the personal data described in this Policy.

2. Data We Collect

Information you provide. When you submit a contact form on the Website, we collect:

  • your first and last name;
  • your email address and, optionally, your Telegram username;
  • whether you contact us as a creator or a company, and the services you are interested in;
  • the content of your request;
  • any file you choose to attach (for example, a CV, brief, media kit or campaign details).

Technical information. When you visit the Website, our hosting provider automatically processes technical data such as your IP address, browser type, device information and the pages requested. This is necessary to deliver the Website and keep it secure.

The Website does not use advertising or analytics cookies and does not track you across other websites.

3. How We Use Your Data

We use your personal data to:

  • respond to your request and communicate with you;
  • evaluate potential cooperation with brands and creators;
  • review job applications and consider you for current or future roles;
  • operate, maintain and protect the Website, including preventing spam and abuse.

We process your data on the basis of your request and the steps you ask us to take before entering into a contract, our legitimate interest in running our business and responding to enquiries, and — where required — your consent.

4. Sharing Your Data

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only with trusted service providers who help us operate the Website and handle your requests, and only to the extent necessary:

  • Vercel Inc. — website hosting and delivery;
  • Resend — delivery of contact form submissions to our inbox;
  • Google — our email service, and web fonts loaded by the Website;
  • jsDelivr — content delivery network for scripts used by the Website.

We may also disclose personal data where required by law or to protect our legal rights. Some of these providers may process data outside your country, including in the United States; in such cases we rely on appropriate safeguards, such as the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses.

5. How Long We Keep Your Data

We keep information from your requests for as long as needed to handle them and for up to 24 months afterwards, unless a longer period is required for an ongoing cooperation or by law. Job applications may be kept for up to 24 months to consider you for future roles — you can ask us to delete your application at any time. Technical logs are kept by our hosting provider for a limited period in line with its policies.

6. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you;
  • ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete data;
  • ask us to delete your data;
  • restrict or object to certain processing;
  • receive your data in a portable format;
  • withdraw your consent at any time, where processing is based on consent;
  • lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us as described below. We will respond within the time required by applicable law.

7. Security

We take appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your personal data against unauthorised access, loss or misuse. Data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS). However, no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Children

The Website is not intended for children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their personal data. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will delete it.

9. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be published on this page with a new “Last updated” date.

10. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how we handle your data, please contact us via the form on our Contact page. See also our Terms of Use.
